NOT A SCREENSAVER WITH A PERSONALITY

What Moth
actually does.

The old project started with a simple conviction: a companion should have a past, a body, and a trustworthy relationship to its world. Every feature here maps to actual code rather than a fantasy feature list.

✿
01

Remembers your history.

Conversation survives restarts. Memories are pinned when you ask, and you can inspect or erase them. No server account required.

☼
02

Helps with your day.

Keep a local task list, close out real commitments, and share a quiet focus session—useful company, not a productivity costume.

♫
03

Talks, listens, adapts.

Local voice output and opt-in microphone input. Connect Ollama on your computer for open-ended conversation; basic offline replies remain available.

⌁
04

Moves real hardware.

Connect a flashed Arduino-compatible controller over USB. Bounded nod, tilt, and ear actions drive actual servos with neutral watchdog recovery.

◈
05

Checks sensor evidence.

A separate Raspberry Pi capture utility signs file hashes at the source device. Moth verifies signatures and distinguishes trusted from unknown keys.

❋
06

Stays in your hands.

Export your nest as JSON. Verify the local signed history. No tracking, forced subscription, hidden cloud server, or invented fleet telemetry.

THE POINT OF THE ORIGINAL YAKFURBY IDEA

Proof begins
before the phone.

Signing whatever the browser receives cannot prove a recording is original. The September 2025 concept insisted on a Raspberry Pi and microcontroller next to the actual sensor. We've brought that requirement back into the design.

Cryptographic honesty matters.

The current Pi utility can sign a local capture's exact bytes. It cannot yet prove secure sensor custody, immutable acquisition firmware, or a trusted physical timestamp. Those are the next hardware milestones—not hidden behind a green checkmark.

A PERSONAL SPACE, NOT A PAPERWORK APP

Where a little presence becomes useful.

The companion lives in a calm desktop environment, with a physically expressive character and a real conversation at the center. Memory, daily actions, hardware and witness receipts stay clear and discoverable.

It is a compiled Windows program that starts a private localhost service and opens a dedicated Edge application window, rather than a mere public website pretending to be installed software.
